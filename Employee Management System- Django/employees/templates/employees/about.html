About Our Employee Management System

A simple Django application designed to manage employee information efficiently.

What is this system?

This Employee Management System allows organizations to maintain and manage employee information in one place.

Employee records include important information such as name, department, branch, city, email address and phone number.

What can you do?

Employee Management

View and manage employee information from a centralized system.

Employee Details

View detailed information about individual employees.

Organized Information

Keep department, branch and contact information organized.

Technology

This application is built using Django and follows Django's Model-View-Template architecture.
